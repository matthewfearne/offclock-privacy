Privacy Policy — Offclock

Effective date: 8 September 2026
App: Offclock (com.offclock.app)
Developer: The Fearne of Life
Contact: thefearneoflife@gmail.com

Offclock does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal information. Everything you enter — your shift pattern, your sleep times, your preferences — is stored only on your own device and never leaves it. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

What the app stores (on your device only)

Offclock saves the following on your phone so it can work out your plan and remember it between sessions. All of it lives in your device's private app storage and is accessible only to Offclock:

  • Your rota — the shift pattern you pick, your shift start and end times, and which day of the cycle you are currently on.
  • Your sleep answers — the times you say you usually fall asleep and wake on your days off, whether you use an alarm on those days, and roughly how much sleep you get on work days.
  • Your sleep log — if you use it, the times you record actually getting to sleep and waking. This is used only to sharpen the timings the app gives you.
  • Your preferences — how you get home after a shift and how long it takes, how caffeine affects you, whether you want to shift fully onto nights or hold a middle ground, your reminder settings, and your dark/light and text size choices.
  • App state — the date you first opened the app (used to work out how much of the free trial is left) and whether you have bought the unlock.

We cannot see any of this. It is never uploaded anywhere, and there is nowhere for us to upload it to.

Information we do NOT collect

To be completely clear, Offclock does not:

  • Require an account, login, or email address.
  • Send your rota, your sleep times, or anything else you enter to us or to anyone else.
  • Collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • Contain advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • Include third-party trackers or advertising SDKs.
  • Access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, calendar, or messages.
  • Use body sensors, your step counter, or Health Connect.
  • Read data from any wearable or fitness tracker.

When the app does use the internet

Offclock has no servers and makes no network requests of its own. Your data is never transmitted.

There is one exception worth being clear about. Offclock offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock the full forward plan, and that purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. When you buy the unlock, or when the app checks whether you already own it, Google Play connects to Google's servers to confirm this. That exchange is between your device and Google, and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

We never see and never receive your payment details. All we ever learn is whether the unlock is owned, and even that is handled on your device rather than reported to us. None of your rota or sleep information is involved in a purchase in any way.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications — so the app can remind you when to wind down, when to get daylight, when to put sunglasses on, and when to stop drinking coffee. You can decline this and the app still works; you would just need to open it to see your plan. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings, individually or all at once.
  • Run at startup — reminders are scheduled as local alarms, and Android clears those when the phone restarts. This permission lets the app put them back after a reboot so you do not silently lose your reminders. It is used for nothing else.

Those are the only two permissions the app asks you for.

The installed app also declares four permissions it never prompts about, all of which come from the Google Play Billing component and exist so the one-time unlock can work:

  • Internet and network state — required by Play Billing. Offclock itself makes no network requests; it has no servers to call. See the section above.
  • Billing — lets the app ask Google Play about the unlock.
  • A private receiver permission that Android generates for the app's own internal use. It grants access to nothing outside the app.

We list these because they show up if you inspect the app, and finding an undeclared internet permission in something that calls itself offline would rightly make you suspicious. Offclock requests nothing beyond this.

Health information

The times you enter are about sleep, so we treat them as sensitive even though they never leave your phone. They are used only to calculate the guidance the app shows you. They are not shared, not sold, not used for advertising, and never used to make any decision about employment, insurance, or credit.

Offclock is a general wellness app. It gives guidance on sleep and light timing based on published research about body-clock rhythms. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. If you are struggling with your sleep or your health, please speak to a doctor or other qualified healthcare professional.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you are always in full control of it:

  • Answer the questions again — in Settings, this replaces your existing plan.
  • Clear app data — in Android Settings → Apps → Offclock → Storage → Clear data. This removes everything the app has stored.
  • Uninstall the app — this deletes all of it permanently.

There is no server-side copy to request, and nothing for us to delete on your behalf, because we never had it. Your purchase record is held by Google Play rather than by the app, so reinstalling and tapping Restore purchase in Settings will restore your unlock.

Children

Offclock is intended for adults who work shifts. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever collected or transmitted data — which is not the plan — this policy would be updated before that version was released.
